Terms & Conditions

Please read this before ordering. This is a student business project, so we've kept things short and plain-language.

1. Who we are

Pop & Go is a small, student-run popcorn business operating at the University of Venda, Thohoyandou campus, run as part of an academic project.

2. Simulated payments

This website uses a simulated payment system for demonstration purposes only. No real money is ever charged, and no real card, EFT, or mobile-money details are collected or processed. Every "payment" you make on this site creates a demo order record in our database and returns a receipt, but no funds move anywhere.

3. Orders & fulfilment

Placing an order through this website does not guarantee same-day physical collection. To actually collect a bag of popcorn on campus, please also check our WhatsApp Status for our daily trading location, or contact us directly.

4. Pricing

Prices shown are in South African Rand (R) and are the same for every customer. We may update prices from time to time; the price shown at checkout is the price that applies to that order.

5. Food safety

All popcorn is prepared in a clean space with gloves and proper ingredient storage. If you have any food allergies or dietary concerns, please contact us before ordering.

6. Your information

If you create an account or check out, we store your name and (optionally) email address against your order so we can issue a receipt and keep basic sales records for the business. We don't sell or share your information with third parties.

7. Changes

We may update these terms as the project develops. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated terms.
