Our Story

Six students, one fresh idea

Pop & Go was created to give the University of Venda community an affordable, freshly made snack option — while giving our team real, hands-on business experience.

Our Mission

Affordable snacks, built for campus

Pop & Go is a small-scale food retail business — simple, repeatable steps from raw ingredient to finished sale. By keeping production costs low and prices fair, we aim to build a small, sustainable business that covers its own costs and grows over time.

Every bag is priced to comfortably cover what it costs us to make, with a healthy margin reinvested into better equipment, more stock, and wider reach. See our pricing page for what each flavour sells for.

What we stand for

Our values

Food Safety & Hygiene

Clean prep area, gloves, and proper storage of ingredients at all times.

Honest & Fair Pricing

No false claims — prices are clear, consistent, and the same for everyone.

Responsible Sourcing

We buy ingredients from legitimate, registered suppliers and reduce waste.

Fair Teamwork

Transparent profit-sharing and an equal say for all six team members.

Respect for Customers

We honour our promises and handle any complaints fairly and promptly.

Campus Approval

We trade with proper permission from the university authorities.

Primary Objective

Build a sustainable business
